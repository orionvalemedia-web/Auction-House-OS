# Acquisition

Devadex Auction House OS is available for acquisition, licensing, and partnership discussions.

---

## What is on offer

Outright transfer of ownership of the product, sold as is.

There is no ongoing maintenance or support obligation, and no licence-back is required, so the
seller keeps nothing that depends on it. It is a clean break.

## What this repository is for

So the asset can be assessed before anyone commits to anything. The intent is that a technical
evaluator can reach a real view, favourable or not, from what is published here.

Start with [PRODUCTS.md](PRODUCTS.md) for what is included and [VERIFICATION.md](VERIFICATION.md)
for what has been measured.

## Available under a signed non-disclosure agreement

- The full product inventory, at file level
- Provenance and chain of title
- Third-party notices and the licence position
- The complete verification record
- The open-items document, listing every known gap at every severity
- Source review, at the appropriate stage of a conversation

## Not published here

Pricing, valuations, sale terms, and the internal risk documents are deliberately absent. They
are part of the data room, not of a public repository.
